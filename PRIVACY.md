# Bunker14 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Bunker14 is a free co-op zombie game for PC and Meta Quest, made by Wolden. This page explains what the game sends over the internet and why. Short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

## What the game sends, and why

**When you play online (2-player lobbies):**
- **Your nickname** (whatever you type in the Name box), your character's look, and your position in the game world, so the other player can see you.
- **Your voice**, if you allow microphone access. Voice is sent live to the other player in your lobby for proximity chat. It is **not recorded or stored**, by the game or the developer.
- **Text chat and direct messages** you send to other players. These are passed through to the other player and are not stored by the developer.

**Every time the game is online (any mode):**
- **A small "last seen" record:** your nickname, a random player ID the game created on your device (looks like `ABCD-1234`, not linked to your real identity), your platform (Windows, Linux or Quest), the game build, and the time. The developer uses this to see how many people play and to enforce bans.
- **Presence for friends and the players-online counter:** your nickname, player ID, and whether you are in a lobby.

**When the game starts:**
- The game checks **GitHub** (github.com) for a newer version and downloads small update files if there is one. GitHub may log the request (IP address, time) under its own privacy policy. No personal data is sent.

## Where it goes

All online traffic goes through the game's own relay server, hosted by **EMQX Cloud** (EMQ Technologies), over an encrypted connection (WSS/TLS). Nothing goes through public servers. Update checks go to **GitHub**.

## Reports and bans

If you press **Report player**, a report is sent to the developer with both players' nicknames and player IDs, the lobby code and the time. The developer may ban a player ID from online play. **Block player** works on your device only: you stop hearing that player's voice and seeing their chat, and they can't join your lobbies.

## What is stored on your device

Your nickname, look, settings, friends list, blocked players and save files are stored only on your own device.

## Microphone

The game asks for microphone permission only for proximity voice chat. You can refuse, or mute yourself at any time with the Microphone button (or the M key on PC). The game works fine without it.

## Children

Bunker14 contains violence and online chat and is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

- Play **solo** (1 player) to skip voice, chat and game data entirely. Solo still sends the "last seen" record, your online presence for friends, and the update check.
- To have your "last seen" record and any reports about you deleted, post a request on the game page (wold3n.itch.io/bunker14) with your friend code (shown next to your name on the main menu), and it will be removed.
